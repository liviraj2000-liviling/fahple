About Fahple

A better way to connect talent and opportunity.

Fahple is designed as a Sri Lankan hiring platform that brings job discovery, candidate profiles and employer workflows into one experience.

What we focus on

Simple tools, useful information and a smoother journey for both sides of the hiring process.

For job seekers

Build a strong profile, discover opportunities and keep your career information organized.

For employers

Find relevant candidates and make hiring more structured and efficient.

For Sri Lanka

Create a platform experience tailored to local job seekers and employers.

Our vision

Make finding meaningful work and finding the right talent simpler, faster and more transparent.

Our approach

Keep the experience practical: useful profiles, searchable opportunities and clear next steps.
